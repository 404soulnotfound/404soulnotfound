i'm a 4th year CSE student at VIT, specialising in AI & ML.

i build things that make machines smarter — models that predict, systems that detect, assistants that think. right now i'm deep into **computer vision**, **nlp**, **rag pipelines** and whatever rabbit hole curiosity pulls me into next.

i also have 254+ leetcode problems solved, which either says something about my dedication or my sleep schedule. probably both.

---

### the origin story 🦾

it started with harry potter.

i built **marauder's map** — a graph ml project on the harry potter universe. gcn allegiance classifier, louvain community detection, snape flagged as a mathematical anomaly. it was equal parts absurd and technically satisfying.

then came **intellisql** — an ai web app that lets anyone query a database in plain english, no sql knowledge needed. you type a question, gemini pro writes the sql, executes it, and explains the result. built it as part of a generative ai course with google.

somewhere between those two projects i started keeping a second brain — a personal knowledge dump of everything i was learning. notes, snippets, ideas at 2am. and then i thought: *what if this brain could talk back?*

that's where the **FRIDAY** idea came from. partly from the second brain. partly because i've watched iron man too many times and tony stark's ai assistant lives rent-free in my head. still building it. still obsessed with it. running on coffee and curiosity.
